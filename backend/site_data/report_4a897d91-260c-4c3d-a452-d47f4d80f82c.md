- 2.5D interposers (SiON/Si, glass) integrating photonic and electronic chiplets with WDM routing

- Polymer-to-SiN adiabatic coupling for package-level optical I/O
- Machine-learning-driven photonic component design (mixed-pitch grating couplers)
- Chiplet-based optics, optical TSV and 3D waveguide / optical 3DIC concepts
- Disaggregated memory/compute AI system architectures built around co-packaged optical I/O

**Open problems:**
- Thermal crosstalk within the electrical domain and between electrical and optical domains
- Signal and power integrity across wide-and-slow and narrow-and-fast channel links
- Trade-offs in interposer material selection (e.g., glass versus silicon)
- Extending per-lane data rates beyond recently demonstrated 200 Gb/s
- High-yield, low-loss, polarization-independent optical coupling at scale
- Standardization, serviceability and thermal-aware co-design for deployable systems
