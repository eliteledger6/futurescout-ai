**Emerging directions:**
- Post-training expert-level pruning and skipping for MoE deployment
- Predictive expert caching, token scheduling and CPU-offload for single-GPU MoE serving
- Fine-grained expert granularity as a tunable design hyperparameter
- Routing signatures as an interpretability and task-classification signal
- Sparse MoE for vision-language models via decoupled multi-stage training
- Hybrid MoE plus graph networks and agentic reasoning for structured tasks
- Convergence and identifiability theory for softmax and dense-to-sparse gating

**Open problems:**
- Training collapse when converting a dense LLM into a sparse vision-language MoE, driven by modality feature gaps and expert load imbalance
- Memory footprint of many expert modules makes single-device deployment difficult; offloading is limited by inaccurate expert-usage prediction
- Routing mechanisms in sparse transformers remain poorly understood despite their role in expert selection
- Whether conditional routing produces real inference speedups on modern hardware at smaller scales
- Choice of expert size / granularity and identifiability conditions for experts lack settled guidance
- Load balancing without expert collapse in MoE classifiers

**Limitations:**
- Analysis is based on titles and abstracts only; no full texts were read, so quantitative claims are as reported by the authors and unverified.
- Several items are arXiv preprints and are not peer reviewed, including the routing-interpretability, offloading, pruning, theory and dialogue-tracking papers.
- The DeepSeekMoE item has only an author-list abstract in the retrieved record, so no technical claims could be extracted from it.
- Many findings rest on a single paper; only the sparse total-vs-active parameter pattern has corroboration across two independent models.
- Sample is small (12 papers) and biased toward LLM-oriented MoE; vision, multimodal and non-LLM applications are represented by one paper each.
- Reported speedups, memory savings and benchmark scores come from heterogeneous hardware, baselines and settings and are not directly comparable.
