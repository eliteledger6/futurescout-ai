Longer chains are more error-prone, and real-hardware noise accumulates faster than the independent-noise baseline predicts, so the embedding overhead is a reliability limit on problem size.
  - source: [Embedding-aware noise modeling of quantum annealing](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11128-026-05293-z) (OpenAlex, 2026-09-16, abstract_only)
  - excerpt: "This embedding overhead limits scalability and reduces reliability as longer chains are more prone to noise-induced errors."
  - reasoning: The abstract reports fitted chain-strength exponents of about 0.8 to 1.0 whose confidence intervals exclude the independent-noise value of 0.5. That points to correlated noise. This is one study on one processor family, and the abstract is truncated before its conclusion.
- **[R2]** *(verified_fact, confidence medium)* On a continuous-variable benchmark, a D-Wave 2000Q matched classical optimizers only within a limited computation-time window, and classical global optimizers did better outside it. The authors' argument for a quantum edge depends on coherence being restored and on noise being suppressed, which is not yet shown on the hardware.
  - source: [Effectiveness of quantum annealing for continuous-variable optimization](https://doi.org/10.1103/physreva.108.042403) (OpenAlex, 2023-10-02, abstract_only)
  - excerpt: "Beyond this domain, classical global optimization algorithms outperform the quantum device."
  - reasoning: The test is a one-dimensional function on an older device generation. The 'potential to significantly outperform' conclusion rests on TEBD, a simulation of ideal coherent annealing, not on hardware results. Simulation-only evidence should not be read as a hardware capability.

Its own introduction concedes that past studies showed no advantage, and the continuous-variable study found classical methods ahead outside a narrow window.
  - source: [Quantum annealing for combinatorial optimization: a benchmarking study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-025-01020-1) (OpenAlex, 2025-05-16, abstract_only)
  - source: [Effectiveness of quantum annealing for continuous-variable optimization](https://doi.org/10.1103/physreva.108.042403) (OpenAlex, 2023-10-02, abstract_only)
