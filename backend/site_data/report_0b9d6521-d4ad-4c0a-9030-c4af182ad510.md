Trimming heaters operating at high temperature raise long-term aging questions that are only extrapolated, not measured.
  - source: [Thermal flux manipulation on the silicon photonic chip to suppress the thermal crosstalk](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0193387) (OpenAlex, 2024-04-01, abstract_only)
