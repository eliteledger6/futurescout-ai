- Missing data: Head-to-head cost and performance comparisons of SFT against RL and on-policy methods

- Missing data: Full-text analysis of papers (findings are largely abstract-level)
- Missing data: Verified confirmation of social-media claims (Huawei, Nous Research, Baidu, KAIST)
- Limitation: The topic phrase count covers only the exact phrase 'supervised fine-tuning', excludes synonyms, and is not normalised for overall growth in publishing.
- Limitation: Patent counts use the broader notion of fine-tuning, include applications that are claims, and recent years are undercounted by publication delay.
- Limitation: Many science findings rest on abstracts of recent preprints with few citations and self-chosen baselines; Risk challenges say several verified_fact labels overstate the evidence.
- Limitation: Market evidence comes from a few filings and keyword matches; the company list is not exhaustive and the next-runner score is a heuristic, not a prediction.
- Limitation: Price findings cover about 100 trading days and are risk context only, not timing guidance.
- Limitation: This is a research assessment, not investment advice.
- Chief review gap: No dedicated science finding on parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA/QLoRA) despite it being a planned query and the dominant practical method for SFT; only tangentially referenced in S8 and patent P2.
