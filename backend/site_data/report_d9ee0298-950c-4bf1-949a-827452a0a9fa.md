- Missing data: Real-world, non-simulated validation of reliability and safety frameworks

- Missing data: Comparison of external enforcement layers with training-based safety approaches
- Missing data: Bibliometric normalisation of paper growth for overall publishing growth
- Missing data: Company evidence for governance and security tooling (no company is named in the cited findings)
- Limitation: Science and risk findings are drawn from a small, search-selected set of mostly abstract-only preprints, so confidence is limited.
- Limitation: Several findings were challenged for overstating generalisability (S7, S9, S10, S5), and their weight is reduced accordingly.
- Limitation: Market findings are company filing excerpts, which describe offerings and risks but not adoption or revenue from agents. Headline- and social-based claims are unverified.
- Limitation: Patent counts reflect claims, not proof, and recent years are undercounted because of publication delay.
- Limitation: Price and timing findings cover about 100 trading days and are used only as risk context, not as timing signals.
- Limitation: This is a research assessment, not investment advice. It gives no price targets, expected returns or recommendations.
- Chief review gap: Limited evidence on actual production deployment performance; most reliability findings (R2, R5, R7) come from benchmarks or simulations rather than live production systems
