# FUTURESCOUT AI: voiceover script (v8, natural presenter)

Written to be spoken, not read: short sentences, one idea each. A blank line between sentences is a short breath
(about 0.35 s); `[pause]` is a longer beat (about 0.8 s). **Bold** words get light emphasis. Scene keys match
`demo/make_video.py`. About 3,000 characters in total (roughly 3 minutes at a medium pace).

Changes in facts since v7: seals are on NEAR **mainnet** now, and reports are **paid only** (no free-after-24-hours).

## 01_intro — Opening

So here's the question we kept asking.

Which technology is about to take off... and what's the actual evidence?

FUTURESCOUT AI answers that with a small team of AI agents, running on **NEAR AI Cloud**.

## 02_radar — Hot-topic radar

This is the radar. It follows fifty-four topics, across nine sectors.

For each one, we count research papers and patent filings. Last year against this year, so far.

That tells you what's heating up right now. Not what was hot last year.

And a paper scout keeps suggesting new topics that are starting to grow.

## 03_agents — The agent team

Pick a topic, and the team gets to work.

A **chief agent** makes the plan. [pause] Then the science, patent, risk and X agents run in parallel, on real papers, real patents and real posts.

The market agent follows the evidence to the companies behind it, and reads their SEC filings. Nobody hands it a list.

The risk agent's job is to argue the other side.

Now, here's the part we're proud of. [pause] The agents run an economy. The trading agent has to **buy** each report from the report agent, in NEAR.

The report agent stakes seventy percent of what it earns with NEAR AI. Those rewards pay for every agent's models.

It keeps the other thirty percent. And once that passes two hundred dollars, it funds a second trading agent.

## 04_report — A research report

Let's open one. High bandwidth memory.

The verdict is strong momentum, with a score of **eighty**.

But look at the confidence. Only fifty percent. Risks lower the confidence, not the score.

Research is up a hundred and six percent this year. Patents, up eighty-one.

## 05_companies — Companies and price check

It names the companies behind that evidence, including some smaller ones.

And it checks how far their share prices have already run. That's a risk check. It's never a buy signal.

## 05b_scout — Company scout

The company scout goes past the big names.

It looks for smaller listed companies behind the patents, and compares their size, growth and research spending.

## 06_evidence — Evidence you can check

Open the full report, and everything is there.

Which agent ran, on which model, and what it cost.

Every finding has a label, and the exact quote from its source. [pause] If a source doesn't back a claim up, a **citation guard** downgrades it.

## 07_seal — Sealed on NEAR

When a report is done, its fingerprint is sealed on **NEAR mainnet**.

The site checks it again every time the page loads. Change a single word, and the seal breaks.

Fund decisions and trades get sealed the same way.

## 09_funds — Funds and trading

Every topic also gets a paper fund, spread across low, medium and high-risk companies, and measured against the S&P 500.

Then there's real money. [pause] A trading agent on a strong model trades tokenized stocks through **NEAR Intents**.

Code checks every order first. The budget, the allowed stocks, the price and the cost.

Only then does the agent approve its own trade.

## 10_round — Real money, end to end

Here's a real round.

The agent woke up on its own. First, it had to buy the report. It paid four point six NEAR for the memory report, straight from its near.com balance.

Then it bought Intel with what was left, and approved the order itself, once every check had passed.

And it's not just our agent. Any trading bot can buy a report, paid in NEAR, and checked on chain.

## 10_close — Close

That's FUTURESCOUT AI. [pause] Research you can check, with models on NEAR AI Cloud, and every report sealed on NEAR.
