## 05_companies — Companies and price check

It names the companies behind that evidence, including some smaller ones.

And it checks how far their share prices have already run. That's a risk check. It's never a buy signal.

## 05b_scout — Company scout

The company scout goes past the big names, to smaller listed companies behind the patents and papers.

It compares their size, growth and research spending, and ranks the ones to watch.

## 06_evidence — Evidence you can check

Open the full report, and everything is there.

Which agents ran, on which model, and what each one cost.

Every finding has a label, and the exact quote from its source.

If a source doesn't back a claim up, a citation guard downgrades it.

## 07_seal — Sealed on NEAR

When a report is done, its fingerprint is sealed on NEAR mainnet. The site checks it again on every page load.

Change a single word, and the seal breaks.

Fund decisions and trades get sealed the same way.
