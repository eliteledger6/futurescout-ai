# FUTURESCOUT AI: demo narration

Video: `futurescout_hackathon_demo.mp4` · 2:55 · 1920x1080 · 30 fps. Voice: Microsoft David Desktop (Windows text-to-speech).

Every app shot is a live recording of the running dashboard. Cards, titles, captions, highlight rings, the mouse pointer, narration and music are added by `demo/make_video.py`.

| Time | Section | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Close | Which technology is about to take off, and what is the evidence? FUTURESCOUT AI answers with a team of AI agents, running on NEAR AI Cloud. |
| 0:08 | 01 Hot-topic radar | The radar tracks 54 topics across nine technology sectors. It ranks them by counting research papers and patent filings: year over year, and this year so far. So you can see which topics are heating up right now. A paper scout also proposes new fast-growing topics in each sector. |
| 0:23 | 02 The agent team | Each topic gets a full research run. A chief agent plans. Science, risk, patent and X agents work in parallel, on real papers, patents and posts. Then the market agent reads the SEC filings of the companies those papers and patents point to. No hand-made company list. An AI check keeps the paper counts on topic, and the risk agent argues the other side. At the end, a fund agent invests a paper fund, and a trading agent trades real tokenized stocks. |
| 0:50 | 03 A research report | Here is the report on high bandwidth memory. The verdict: strong momentum, with an opportunity score of 80. But confidence is only 50%. Risk lowers confidence, not the score. Research is up 106% this year so far, and patents 81%. |
| 1:06 | 04 Companies and price check | It names the companies behind the evidence, including smaller ones. And it checks whether their share prices have already run: a risk check, never a buy signal. |
| 1:15 | 05 Company scout | A company scout looks past the big names, for smaller listed companies behind the patents and papers. It checks their size, growth and research spending, and ranks the next runners. |
| 1:25 | 06 Evidence you can check | The full report opens everything up. The agent trace shows which agents ran, on which model, and at what cost. Every finding carries a label, and the exact quote from its source. A citation guard downgrades anything a source does not support. |
| 1:38 | 07 Sealed on NEAR | Each report's fingerprint is sealed on NEAR testnet, and re-checked on every page load. Change one word, and the seal breaks. Fund decisions and every trade are fingerprinted on NEAR the same way. |
| 1:50 | 08 Funds and trading | Every topic gets a paper fund, fully invested across low, medium and high risk companies. Each paper fund lists the reasons for every decision, and is measured against the S&P 500. A trading agent on a strong model trades tokenized stocks with real money, through NEAR Intents. Code checks every order: budget, allowed stocks, price and cost. Then the agent approves its own trade. In shadow mode it has placed 7,100 pretend dollars at real quotes, and explains each choice. |
| 2:17 | 09 A real trading round | The agent wakes up on its own: on a new report, a sharp price move, or its daily review. Here it reviewed its holdings. Google's report had weakened, so it sold the whole position, swapping it back into NEAR. Seconds later it put the money into Intel, backed by the strongest report: high bandwidth memory. The whole round, sale and purchase, took under a minute, with no human in the loop. Every real trade is listed with the agent's reasons, and its record is sealed on NEAR. |
| 2:44 | Close | FUTURESCOUT AI: evidence-first technology research. Models on NEAR AI Cloud, reports sealed on NEAR. Research information, not financial advice. |
